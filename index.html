Controls & rules

Move with your mouse or finger. Press Space or tap SPLIT to split toward your aim. You may start alone; Player 2 and Player 3 can join later. Bigger player cells can eat smaller rival cells, and bots can eat players too. Full PvP deaths respawn at starting size with a short shield. The arena is 5000×4000.
