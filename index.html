SOLO + 2 PLAYER CO-OP

Blob Buddies

An Agar.io-inspired browser game you can play solo immediately, then seamlessly continue in co-op when a buddy joins.

or

Controls

Move with your mouse or finger. Press Space or tap SPLIT to split toward your aim. You can start playing immediately after creating a room; your buddy may join later with the room code. Once they arrive, press W or tap TRANSFER to donate mass. Eat pellets and smaller enemies and reach 5000 team mass. The arena is 5000×4000.
